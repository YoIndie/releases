# MacDrop changelog

## 1.1.0

- File Shelf: drop files or folders on the notch from any app, switch desktops or apps, then drag them where they need to go. They stay until you remove them.
- Prompts: save the prompts you use often and click one to copy it.
- Notes: quick notes in a list, one click to copy.
- Projects: choose up to three editors or terminals for the buttons on each project (… › Buttons on Projects).
- AI Usage: Codex shows its numbers again, now that the Codex app compresses its logs, and your plan and limits are live.
- Drag a file over a tab's icon to open that tab.

## 1.0.1

- Sales: connecting a store now shows its sales straight away, for Stripe, Shopify, Dodo Payments, Polar and Razorpay. Before, the numbers only appeared after switching tabs.
- Sales: disconnecting a store updates the store list and Settings at once, and disconnecting the last one returns to the welcome screen.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Hover the top of your screen to open MacDrop; on Macs without a notch and on external displays, it lives in the menu bar.
- Clipboard history, with screenshots.
- Ask MacDrop: Apple Intelligence on your Mac (macOS 26).
- AI Usage for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and more.
- Now playing for Apple Music and Spotify.
- Calendar with Join buttons, Reminders and Timers.
- Teleprompter, Sales, Camera and Keep Awake.
- System Explorer, with menu bar readouts.
