# MacDrop changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Hover the top of your screen to open MacDrop; on Macs without a notch and on external displays, it lives in the menu bar.
- Clipboard history, with screenshots.
- Ask MacDrop: Apple Intelligence on your Mac (macOS 26).
- AI Usage for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and more.
- Now playing for Apple Music and Spotify.
- Calendar with Join buttons, Reminders and Timers.
- Teleprompter, Sales, Camera and Keep Awake.
- System Explorer, with menu bar readouts.
